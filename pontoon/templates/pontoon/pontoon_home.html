How Pontoon Works

Choose one numbered football to reveal your hidden World Cup team. Once selected, you cannot change your football.

  • Each goal scored by your team is worth 2 points.
  • Each goal conceded by your team deducts 1 point.
  • If your team goes over 21 points, you are bust.
  • First team to reach 21 points wins.
  • If no team reaches 21 points, the team closest to 21 points wins.
